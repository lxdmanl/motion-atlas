# Third-party sources

- **human-atlas** — https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas, pinned commit `1c38bf35c254a891200d3cedecfd57abebe83d8d`. Application code MIT; see repository LICENSE, which is preserved.
- **BodyParts3D 4.0** — © The Database Center for Life Science, CC BY 4.0. See [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) for the official dataset, license and adaptation details. The right arm here adds skin weights and two labelled schematic tendon paths. Source IDs and source geometry at rest are preserved.
- **MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.0.1** — Google / MediaPipe, Apache-2.0; [license text](licenses/Apache-2.0.txt). Runtime JavaScript/WASM copied without alteration from the pinned npm package. https://www.npmjs.com/package/@mediapipe/tasks-vision/v/1.0.1. Local pose model is Google Pose Landmarker Full float16 v1; its exact source URL, sizes and hashes are in `mediapipe/manifest.json`. Model documentation: https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker.
- **Three.js 0.159.0**, React 19.2.6, lucide-react and remaining npm libraries retain their own MIT/other package licenses. Complete dependency versions are in package-lock.json. The production bundler retains legal notices in its output.
- **Blender 4.5.14** is a GPL-licensed development tool obtained from the Blender Foundation. It is not redistributed in this repository and is not required by the finished browser app.
- **mcp-for-blender 2.1.3** — third-party MIT development tool, https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender. Installed separately under `.tools`, not bundled into the site.

Short anatomical facts are written for this application and linked to references, including Washington University educational pages and OpenStax, *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, §11.5, Rice University: https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-5-muscles-of-the-pectoral-girdle-and-upper-limbs. OpenStax currently identifies its textbook content as CC BY-NC-SA and states separate restrictions for AI use; it must not be described as CC BY 4.0. This repository does not bundle textbook passages, figures, or an OpenStax corpus, or claim a license to reuse those materials in AI products. The short factual catalogue describes anatomical possibilities, not measured activation in a particular user; reference details are in `docs/ANATOMY.md`.
